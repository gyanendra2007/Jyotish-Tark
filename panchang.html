Today's Cosmic Guidance & Cautions

🕉️

Sankashti Chaturthi & Kajari Teej

Today marks Heramba Sankashti Chaturthi and Kajari Teej. The shift from Tritiya to Chaturthi at 08:50 AM makes this an incredibly potent day for fasting and worshiping Lord Ganesha to remove persistent life obstacles. Married women also observe Kajari Teej today for marital bliss and longevity.

🌙

Revati's Nourishment

The Moon transits Pisces under the Revati Nakshatra. As the final Nakshatra of the zodiac, Revati is associated with closure, compassion, safe journeys, and completion. It is a day to reflect on what you need to finish before rushing toward new beginnings.

⚠️

Bhadra & Panchaka Advisory

Be mindful that Vishti Karana (Bhadra Kaal) is active in the morning until 08:50 AM, rendering that window unsuitable for initiating new, auspicious ventures. Additionally, the Panchaka period continues today, advising caution with major household or travel decisions. Plan important meetings for the midday Abhijit Muhurat.
