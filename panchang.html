Bhadra Kaal Warning

Today features a prolonged Bhadra period, starting at 09:08 AM alongside the Purnima Tithi and lasting until approximately 09:32 PM. It is traditional and highly advised not to hold large ceremonies intended to bring good luck, commence major tasks, or perform Raksha Bandhan rituals during this specific window.

🌕

Shravana Purnima Energy

While the full Purnima extends to tomorrow, the Purnima Vrat is observed today, August 27. Chandra (the Moon) symbolizes feelings, family comfort, and the ability to feel financially secure. This makes today an excellent day for spiritual fasting and inner reflection rather than outward expansion.

🧘

Purnima Remedies

To navigate the heavy energy of Bhadra and harness the Moon's power, donate white rice today to someone in real need—a symbolic act tying food security to gratitude. You may also offer water mixed with a little raw milk to the Moon in the evening to pray for emotional stability.
