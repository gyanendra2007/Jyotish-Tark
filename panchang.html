Sarvartha Siddhi Yoga

Today features an incredibly powerful and auspicious alignment called Sarvartha Siddhi Yoga, which lasts practically the entire day. This yoga signifies that efforts and new tasks initiated today are highly likely to be completed successfully. It is a fantastic window for important business negotiations or signing contracts.

☀️

Solar Leadership

Being Ravivara (Sunday) combined with Krishna Dwitiya, the energy naturally favors administrative, governmental, and leadership-oriented planning. If you need to make decisions regarding authority or take a leading role in a project, the Solar energy supports you fully today.

⚠️

Panchaka Advisory

Please note that the Panchaka period is active throughout the entire day. While the Sarvartha Siddhi Yoga cancels out many minor doshas, traditional practices suggest avoiding activities like starting home construction (especially the roof), traveling southward, or collecting wood/fuel during this period.
