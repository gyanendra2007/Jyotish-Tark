Intellectual Clarity

Budhavara (Wednesday) is ruled by Mercury, making it a highly intellectual and communicative day. With the Moon traversing the Shravana Nakshatra—the star of listening—the cosmic environment heavily favors gathering information, studying, and engaging in trade or negotiations.

Auspicious Alignments
