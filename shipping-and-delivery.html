Shipping & Delivery Policy

Jyotish Tark offers digital astrology reports, consultations, and physical rudraksha products. Please review how each is delivered.

Digital Reports (Instant)

  • KP Astrology Report, Numerology Report and Sampurna Kundali — the PDF is generated and opens on the same page immediately after successful payment.
  • Please pay from the same phone and browser where you entered your birth details, so the report can open automatically.
  • If the report does not open, send your payment screenshot or order ID on WhatsApp (+91 8318513814) and we will send the PDF within 24 hours.

Consultations

  • Call, video call and personal-meeting slots are confirmed on WhatsApp after booking.
  • Audio/video consultations are paid only after the call; personal meetings and home visits are by prior appointment.

Rudraksha (Physical Products)

  • Free delivery across India.
  • Dispatch and delivery in about 3–5 days for orders without a certificate.
  • Orders with a lab certificate (INGEMCO) take about 2–4 extra days for testing.
  • Cash on Delivery and online payment (Cashfree) are both available.
  • Delivery details are confirmed on WhatsApp after the order. Remote pin codes may take a little longer.

Non-Receipt or Delay

  • For reports: check that payment was completed, then contact us with your order ID.
  • For rudraksha: if the parcel has not arrived within the time above, contact us with your order ID and we will share the tracking status.
