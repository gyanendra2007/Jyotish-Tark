Cancellation Policy

At Jyotish Tark, we strive to provide the best astrological services. Please read our cancellation policy carefully.

Digital Services

Our reports (Sampurna Kundali, KP, Numerology, Varshphal, Mobile Number and one-question reports) are digital products that open on the website as a PDF once the payment is confirmed. A report order can be cancelled only in these cases:

  • Cancellation requests must be made within 2 hours of purchase
  • If the report has not been generated or delivered yet
  • Technical issues from our side that prevent delivery

Consultation Services

For astrological consultations (see consultation prices):

  • Audio and video calls, and remedies sent by message, are paid in advance online (UPI / Razorpay).
  • A paid consultation cannot be cancelled for a refund, but it can be rescheduled: tell us on WhatsApp before the scheduled time and we will give you a new time.
  • If we are unable to provide a paid consultation at all, you get a full refund (see the Refund Policy).
  • Your first 5-minute call is free (once per mobile number).
  • Personal appointments (₹11,000 in Lucknow, ₹21,000 at your location with no extra travel charge) are paid in advance when the appointment is confirmed.
  • A personal appointment can be rescheduled if you inform us at least 48 hours before the scheduled time.
  • Advance payments for personal appointments are not refundable — only rescheduling is available.

Rudraksha Orders

  • A rudraksha order can be changed or cancelled within 48 hours of placing it, as long as it has not been dispatched.
  • After 48 hours, or once the order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled.
