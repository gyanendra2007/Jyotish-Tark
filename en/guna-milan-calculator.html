What is guna milan (ashtakoot)?

In the Vedic tradition, before marriage the groom’s and bride’s birth nakshatra and moon sign are matched on eight criteria (kootas). Together they carry 36 points — hence the name 36-guna matching.

KootaMax pointsWhat it shows
Varna1Work, ego and spiritual harmony
Vashya2Mutual attraction and influence
Tara3Fortune and well-being together
Yoni4Nature and physical compatibility
Graha Maitri5Thinking and mental match
Gana6Behaviour — Deva, Manushya, Rakshasa
Bhakoot7Love, family and prosperity
Nadi8Well-being and children

How many points should match?

Traditionally below 18 points are not recommended, 18–24 is average, 25–32 good and 33–36 excellent. But even 36 points do not guarantee a happy marriage — the 7th house, dasha and Mars of both charts are also studied.

Nadi dosha and Bhakoot dosha

If both have the same nadi, there is nadi dosha (0/8); if the moon signs are 2-12, 5-9 or 6-8 from each other, there is bhakoot dosha (0/7). The texts also give cancellations — such as the same sign with different nakshatras, or friendly sign lords. The calculator shows these automatically.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need for guna milan?

The date, time and place of birth of both the groom and the bride. These give the Moon’s nakshatra and sign, on which ashtakoot is based.

Can gunas be matched by name?

Without birth details, tradition uses the first letter of the name as the ‘name sign’, but this is less reliable. Matching by birth details is considered correct.

Should we not marry if fewer than 18 points match?

Tradition does not recommend fewer than 18 points, but if nadi and bhakoot are cancelled and both charts are good, astrologers do approve the marriage. Show both full charts.

Why do different websites show different points?

Panchangs differ slightly in the tables for Yoni, Vashya and Gana, and the ayanamsa may differ too. A 1–2 point difference is normal.

Is kundali matching needed for a love marriage?

Not necessary, but many families look at it. Matching shows where more understanding is needed — treat it as guidance, not a verdict on the relationship.
