How is this worked out?

On the 15th of every month, the real sidereal (KP ayanamsa) positions of Jupiter, Saturn, Rahu, Mars, the Sun, Venus and Mercury are taken, and we see which house they are in from your Moon sign . In the classical transit rules some houses are considered good for each planet (for example Jupiter 2, 5, 7, 9, 11; Saturn 3, 6, 11). Jupiter and Saturn get the most weight, because they stay in one sign for a long time.

What is the Personal Year number?

In numerology, the digits of your birth day + month + the current year (2027) are added and reduced to a single digit. This cycle from 1 to 9 shows what the year favours — beginnings, partnerships, change or completion.

Limits

A sign-based transit reading is the same for everyone with that sign. Your own dasha, ascendant and birth planets can change it — a personal forecast needs the full kundali.
