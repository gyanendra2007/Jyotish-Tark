Cancer 2027 — the year in short

In 2027, two big transits matter most for Cancer — Jupiter and Saturn. From January to June, Jupiter is in your own sign — this adds responsibilities, with restlessness of mind and chances of a change of place. But with Jupiter aspecting the 5th, 7th and 9th houses, it helps in matters of children, marriage and fortune. From 26 June to 26 November, Jupiter in the 2nd house strengthens money, family and speech — income rises, savings grow, with signs of happy family events and stuck money coming back. From 26 November, Jupiter moves into Virgo, the 3rd house from your sign.

Until 3 June and again from 20 October, Saturn in the 9th house slows luck a little — more effort is needed; respecting your father and elders, and religious discipline, bring benefits. From 3 June to 20 October, Saturn in Aries, the 10th house, increases workload and responsibility — slow but solid career progress; honesty in your work is your greatest support.

Rahu in the 7th house advises avoiding misunderstandings in partnerships and relationships. Ketu in your sign may bring tiredness and indifference — keep up meditation and a regular routine.

Good news: Cancer is not under Saturn’s Sade Sati or Dhaiya in 2027.

Most favourable months: July, August, September · Months to be careful: February, March

Cancer 2027 — results for every area of life

Career and job

  • Between June and November, Jupiter strengthens your career house (10th) with its aspect — new responsibility, promotion or new job opportunities come up during this time.
  • Saturn’s transit of the 10th house increases work pressure — keep in step with your boss and seniors, avoid shortcuts.

Money and finances

  • From June to November, Jupiter is in the 2nd house — income will rise; good chances of savings and of stuck money coming back.

Love and marriage

  • From January to June, Jupiter aspects your house of marriage (7th) — good chances of a match being fixed for the unmarried, and of sweetness in marriage for the married.
  • From January to June, Jupiter influences the 5th house — stability in love and good news about children.
  • With Saturn influencing the 7th house (from 3 June to 20 October), patience and understanding are needed in relationships — marriage talks may move a little slowly.
  • Because of Rahu-Ketu, avoid misunderstandings in relationships — speak clearly.

Family

  • Signs of auspicious events in the family, a new member’s arrival or celebrations.

Health

  • Avoid mental stress and an irregular routine because of Rahu-Ketu.

Education and competitive exams

  • This is a good year for studies and competitive exams — Jupiter’s link with the 5th/9th house gives focus and success.

Remedies for Cancer in 2027

  • For the lord of your sign: Offer water and milk to Lord Shiva on Mondays — this is your main remedy for the whole year.
  • Thursday practice: On Thursdays offer water to a banana tree, donate yellow lentils or turmeric, and take the blessings of teachers.
  • For Rahu: On Saturdays feed grain to birds; stay away from intoxicants and lies; chant "Om Ram Rahave Namah" (ॐ रां राहवे नमः) 108 times.
  • For Ketu: Worship Ganesh Ji and feed dogs; chant "Om Kem Ketave Namah" (ॐ कें केतवे नमः).
