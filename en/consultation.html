Frequently asked questions

What does “first 5 minutes free” mean?

First-time callers get one free 5-minute call — once per mobile number. Tell us your concern; for a detailed discussion choose any paid call.

When do I pay?

Except for the free first call, every consultation is paid in advance. In India you can pay by UPI; from abroad we share bank details on WhatsApp.

Can I change my appointment date?

Yes. In-person appointments can be moved with 48 hours’ notice. The advance payment is not refunded — only the date can be changed.

What should I keep ready for the call?

Your date of birth, the exact time of birth (as accurate as possible), place of birth, and your two or three main questions.

Does a consultation guarantee results?

No. Astrology offers guidance, not guarantees. For health, legal or financial decisions please consult a doctor, lawyer or financial adviser. Full terms: Terms · Cancellation · Refund
