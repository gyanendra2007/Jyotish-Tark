Frequently asked questions

Which sign should I read the weekly horoscope for?

In Vedic astrology the horoscope is read from the Moon sign (birth sign). If you don’t know your sign, Free Kundali make one, or name-letter table to find your name sign.

How is this horoscope made?

From this week’s real planetary transits (KP ayanamsa, Lucknow): which house the Sun, Mars, Mercury, Venus, Jupiter, Saturn and Rahu are in from your sign, and which house the Moon is in each day. Good days are when the Moon is in the 1, 3, 6, 7, 10 or 11th house from your sign.

What are the days to be careful?

When the Moon is in the 8th house from your sign (Chandrashtam), tradition advises putting off big decisions, travel and disputes.

Will this be equally right for everyone?

These are general results based on the sign (transits). Personal results depend on the dasha and planets of your own kundali — show your kundali for that.
