Frequently asked questions

What if I don't know my birth time?

Without the birth time the lagna and houses cannot be fixed. With the dates of 3–4 major life events the astrologer can do birth-time rectification — mention it on WhatsApp when you order.

How is it different from the free kundali?

The free kundali gives the lagna chart, planets, panchang and a summary of the main areas of life. The full report covers every topic in detail — divisional charts, likely timing of marriage, dasha dates, varshphal 2027 and remedies.

Does the report guarantee the future?

No. Astrology gives guidance, not guarantees. For health, legal or financial decisions please consult the right professional.
