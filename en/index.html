FAQ

Common questions

Is the birth chart really free?

Yes. The online kundali with lagna chart, navamsa, planets, dasha and a 12-chapter reading is free, and you can save it as a PDF on your phone. Paid options are the 35+ page Full Kundali Report (₹1,101) and consultations.

Can I get my reading in English?

Yes. Choose “English” as the report language on the kundali page. Consultations are available in English or Hindi.

I live outside India. How do I pay?

Prices are in Indian rupees. If you have an Indian UPI account you can pay directly; otherwise message us on WhatsApp and we will share bank details. Your report or call link is sent on WhatsApp.

Does astrology guarantee results?

No. Astrology offers guidance, not guarantees. For health, legal or financial decisions please consult a qualified doctor, lawyer or financial adviser.
