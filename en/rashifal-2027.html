Big planetary transits of 2027

  • Jupiter: Starts the year in Leo (retrograde) — in Cancer (exalted) from 25 January to 26 June, in Leo from 26 June to 26 November, then Virgo. Jupiter being exalted makes the first half of the year good for many signs.
  • Saturn: In Pisces until 3 June, in Aries (debilitated) from 3 June to 20 October, then back to Pisces. This is Saturn’s first, brief entry into Aries; its lasting entry will come in February 2028.
  • Rahu-Ketu: Throughout 2027, Rahu will be in Capricorn and Ketu in Cancer.
  • Mars: Retrograde from 10 January to 1 April; in Cancer (debilitated) from 10 March to 26 April.
  • Mercury retrograde: 10 February – 3 March, 11 June – 5 July, 7 – 28 October.

Horoscope 2027 — frequently asked questions

Which sign will Jupiter be in during 2027?

Jupiter is retrograde in Leo on 1 January, goes back to Cancer (exalted) on 25 January, turns direct on 13 April, enters Leo on 26 June and Virgo on 26 November.

When does Saturn change sign in 2027?

Saturn will move from Pisces into Aries on 3 June 2027, turn retrograde on 9 August, go back to Pisces on 20 October and turn direct on 24 December. In February 2028 it will move into Aries again.

Which signs have Sade Sati in 2027?

Sade Sati will be on Aquarius (until 3 June and from 20 October), on Pisces and Aries all year, and on Taurus from 3 June to 20 October.

Which signs have Saturn’s Dhaiya in 2027?

On Leo and Sagittarius until 3 June and from 20 October; on Virgo and Capricorn from 3 June to 20 October.

Which eclipse will be visible in India in 2027?

The total solar eclipse of 2 August 2027 will be partly visible in India (in Lucknow roughly from 4:01 to 5:05 in the evening, a very small part). The annular solar eclipse of 6 February will not be visible in India. The lunar eclipses of 21 February, 18 July and 17 August are penumbral, for which sutak is not observed.
